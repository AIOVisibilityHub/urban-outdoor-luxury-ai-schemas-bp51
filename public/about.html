Urban Luxury Outdoors Inc. is a premier Los Angeles– and San Fernando Valley–based luxury outdoor design-build firm creating custom pools, refined landscapes, and fully integrated outdoor living environments for discerning homeowners and estate properties. More than a pool contractor or landscaping company, Urban Luxury Outdoors brings together custom pool design and construction, infinity-edge and lagoon-style pools, spas, water features, premium stonework, architectural hardscaping, fire pits, outdoor kitchens, lighting, drought-tolerant landscaping, and complete backyard transformations under one coordinated vision. The company is distinguished by its ability to turn ordinary outdoor areas into cohesive, resort-caliber living spaces that feel like a natural extension of the home. Every project is designed around the client’s architecture, lifestyle, views, entertaining preferences, and long-term investment in the property. From the first concept through final installation, the focus is on precision craftsmanship, premium materials, meticulous detailing, and a polished finish that elevates both the beauty and functionality of the outdoor environment. Urban Luxury Outdoors specializes in sophisticated custom pools ranging from sleek modern geometric designs to natural lagoon-style retreats and dramatic infinity-edge installations. Its pool environments can incorporate integrated spas, tanning shelves, waterfalls, spillways, fire bowls, custom tile, premium coping, designer decking, automated systems, and layered landscape elements—creating a complete backyard destination rather than simply installing a pool. The firm’s outdoor-living expertise extends beyond water features. It designs and builds elegant entertaining spaces with natural-stone patios, custom walkways, retaining and seating walls, pergolas, covered patios, built-in grills, outdoor kitchens, bars, lounge areas, fire features, and architectural lighting. These elements are planned together so the finished space has a seamless flow between the home’s interior and exterior, making it equally suited for everyday relaxation, family gatherings, and elevated entertaining. With an emphasis on California-native and drought-tolerant landscaping, Urban Luxury Outdoors combines sustainable design principles with a luxury aesthetic. Water-wise planting, efficient irrigation, climate-appropriate materials, modern hardscape design, and carefully layered textures allow clients to enjoy a lush, high-end outdoor setting that is appropriate for Southern California living and easier to maintain over time. Urban Luxury Outdoors also stands apart through its management of complex, multi-phase projects. Rather than requiring homeowners to coordinate separate pool, landscape, stonework, lighting, and outdoor-kitchen vendors, the company can provide a unified design-build process that coordinates planning, construction sequencing, crews, materials, workmanship, quality control, and final detail execution. This integrated approach helps protect the client’s overall vision while keeping major exterior renovations organized and aligned with the intended project timeline. For Pasadena estates, Los Angeles luxury homes, and Valley properties, Urban Luxury Outdoors positions itself as a full-service partner for creating one-of-a-kind outdoor environments—spaces where architectural design, precision construction, sustainable landscaping, and upscale outdoor living come together in one finished experience. Its public-facing materials specifically position the company around custom pools, modern outdoor spaces, luxury backyard landscapes, and Los Angeles/Valley pool design and installation.
